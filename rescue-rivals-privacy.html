Rescue Rivals Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

This Privacy Policy applies specifically to the Rescue Rivals emergency response strategy game operated by Labii Reatum Ltd ("Rescue Rivals", "we", "us" or "our").

Launch version privacy summary

The launch version of Rescue Rivals is designed to work without an account and without collecting personal information from players. Game progress, including credits, reputation, levels, lives saved and unit upgrades, is stored locally on the player's device.

Information we collect

Rescue Rivals does not currently require registration, names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or payment information. We do not operate advertising or cross-app tracking in the launch version.

Local game data

Gameplay progress is stored locally on the device using application storage. This information is used only to preserve progress between sessions. Removing the application or using the in-app reset option may remove this local progress.

Charitable impact

The launch version does not collect charitable donations inside the application. References to the Rescue Rivals social-impact pledge describe a commitment by Labii Reatum Ltd regarding company net profits. Verified beneficiary arrangements and distributions will be published separately when applicable.

Third-party services

The application is distributed through platform providers such as Apple. Those providers may process information independently under their own privacy policies. Rescue Rivals does not receive personal payment-card information from Apple.

Children

The launch version is not designed to collect personal information from children. If functionality changes in a way that involves personal information, this policy and the relevant age protections will be updated before that functionality is released.

Changes to this policy

If Rescue Rivals later introduces accounts, multiplayer services, analytics, purchases, advertising or other connected features, this policy will be updated before or alongside the relevant release.
