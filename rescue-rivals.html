An emergency services game built around decisions, not tapping

Every incident asks a simple question: what is the right response? Send too little and the emergency can overwhelm your teams. Send too much and other parts of your district may be left exposed. Rescue Rivals combines emergency dispatch gameplay with fleet building, upgrades and long-term strategy.

Rescue Rivals FAQ

What kind of game is Rescue Rivals?

Rescue Rivals is an emergency response strategy and simulation game for mobile. You command fire, ambulance, police and specialist rescue services, respond to incidents and build your emergency response network.

Can I play as a firefighter, paramedic or police dispatcher?

You play as the overall incident commander rather than one individual worker. Your job is to decide which emergency services and vehicles should respond to each situation.

What emergencies are in Rescue Rivals?

The launch build includes incidents such as house fires, motorway collisions, warehouse fires, missing-person searches, flash floods and major city-centre incidents, with more scenarios planned.

Is Rescue Rivals an official emergency service app?

No. Rescue Rivals is a fictional strategy game created by Labii Reatum Ltd. It does not provide real-world emergency guidance and is not affiliated with an emergency service unless a future partnership is explicitly announced.

Does Rescue Rivals support emergency service charities?

The game is being built around a company pledge to direct 50% of net profits to emergency service charities. Verified beneficiary arrangements and distributions will be published transparently when applicable.
