Rescue Rivals FAQ

What kind of game is Rescue Rivals?

Rescue Rivals is an emergency response strategy and simulation game for mobile. You command fire, ambulance, police and specialist rescue services, complete story missions and build a larger response network.

Can I play as a firefighter, paramedic or police dispatcher?

You play as the overall incident commander rather than one individual worker. Your job is to decide which emergency services and vehicles should respond to each fictional situation.

Does Rescue Rivals have a story mode?

Yes. The launch campaign contains six chapters and 24 connected missions, supported by recurring Harbour City Command characters. Endless Operations remain available beyond the campaign.

Is Rescue Rivals suitable for younger and older players?

The game is designed around clear controls and family-friendly fictional emergencies. Guided mode helps new players learn the response system, while Standard and Expert modes add tighter strategy for experienced players.

Is Rescue Rivals an official emergency service app?

No. Rescue Rivals is a fictional strategy game created by Labii Reatum Ltd. It does not provide real-world emergency guidance and is not affiliated with an emergency service unless a future partnership is explicitly announced.
