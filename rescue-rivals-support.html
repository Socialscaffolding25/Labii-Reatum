Launch version

The launch build stores gameplay progress locally on your device. There is no account or cloud save in the first release. Deleting the app or resetting local progress may permanently remove that progress.

Useful information to include

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • Your Rescue Rivals app version.
  • A short description of what happened and what you expected to happen.
  • A screenshot where helpful, provided it does not contain information you do not want to share.
